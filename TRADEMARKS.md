# Trademarks

Forma's code is MIT-licensed: use it, change it, fork it and sell it. The
licence covers the code, not the name "Forma" or its logo, which belong to
German Molina. This page says how they may be used, so that people can tell
the official project from everything built on it.

## Fine without asking

- Saying that something uses, runs on, works with or is built on Forma
  ("built with Forma", "a Forma agent", "Forma hosting by Acme").
- Writing, teaching and talking about Forma, with the logo, unmodified, to
  refer to the project.
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under their name.

## Needs permission

- A modified version, or a fork, distributed or offered as a service under the
  name Forma or a confusingly similar one. Forks are welcome: give them their
  own name, and say they are based on Forma.
- "Forma" or the logo in the name of a product, service, company or domain.
- Anything that suggests the project endorses, certifies or is affiliated with
  you, when it does not.

## Never

- The name or logo on anything that deceives or harms people (scams, spam,
  malware, impersonation), or that breaks the law.

Ask at germolinal@gmail.com. Permission can be withdrawn, and any use that
breaks these rules has to stop.
